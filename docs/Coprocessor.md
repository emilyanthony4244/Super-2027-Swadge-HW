| 6 | 3V3 | --- |

| 8 | Unused | --- |
| 9 | BTN_4 "Down" | --- |
| 10 | Unused | --- |
| 11 | VMON | ADC |
| 12 | CHARGE | --- |
| 13 | STANDBY | --- |
| 14 | BTN_6 "MENU" | --- |
| 15 | CH32_COMMS | --- |

| 17 | BTN_8 "B" | --- |
| 18 | BTN_7 "A" | --- |
